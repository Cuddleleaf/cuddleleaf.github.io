Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have - in plain words.

Last updated 13 November 2026

Our online store is opening soon, and this policy covers it. On our Coming Soon page, the “Notify me” form sends the email address you type to us through Web3Forms, a form service, so we can let you know when the store opens.

Who we are

This website is run by Cuddle Leaf Australia (ABN 13 768 012 913), a small Australian business. In this policy "we", "us" and "our" mean Cuddle Leaf Australia.

We respect your privacy and handle your personal information with care, guided by the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it and the choices you have. If anything is unclear, write to us at contact.cuddleleaf@gmail.com.

What we collect

We only collect what we need to run the store and look after your order.

  • When you create an account: your name, email address, a password and, if you choose to give them, your mobile number and the delivery addresses you save.
  • When you place an order: your name, email address, mobile number (optional), delivery address, what you ordered and any note you add.
  • When you build a gift box: the occasion, the recipient’s name, who it is from and your gift message.
  • When you leave a review: the name you choose to show, your star rating, your comments and any photos you upload.
  • When you join our mailing list: your email address.
  • When you contact us: whatever you tell us by email, in the messages in your account, or on social media.

We do not collect or store card details on this website. Online card payment is not available yet. When it is, payments will be handled by a payment provider and we will update this policy before it starts.

How we use it

We use your information to:

  • prepare, pack and deliver your order, and arrange payment for it;
  • send you updates about your order by email, and by WhatsApp or text message if you gave us your mobile number;
  • run your account, including your order history and invoices;
  • answer your questions and put things right if something goes wrong;
  • show your review on the product you bought;
  • send you news and offers, but only if you joined our mailing list;
  • keep the store secure, prevent misuse and meet our legal and tax obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for anything unrelated to the reasons above.

Who we share it with

We share your information only with those who help us serve you, and only as much as they need:

  • Delivery partners, such as Australia Post, who need the recipient’s name, address and contact details to deliver a parcel.
  • Service providers who host and run this website, and who provide our email and document storage (Google, including Google Drive, where we keep a copy of each invoice for our records) and messaging (WhatsApp, a Meta service).
  • Professional advisers and authorities, such as our accountant or a government agency, where the law requires or allows it.

Some of these providers, including Google and Meta, store information on servers outside Australia. When we use them, we take reasonable steps to make sure your information is handled properly.

Reviews and photos

Reviews are public. The name you choose, your rating, your comments and your photos appear on the product page for anyone to see, marked as coming from a verified buyer. We never show your email address, your delivery address or your order number with a review.

Please only upload photos you are happy to share publicly and have the right to share, and take care with photos that show a child’s face. If you would like a review or photo taken down, write to us and we will remove it.

Sending a gift to someone else

If you send an order to another person, you give us their name and address so we can deliver it. Please make sure they would be comfortable with that. We use a gift recipient’s details only to deliver the gift and do not contact them for marketing.

Cookies and your browser

We keep this simple. When you sign in, we place one essential cookie in your browser so the website knows it is you. It cannot be read by scripts on the page and expires when you sign out or after a set time. Your browser also remembers your name and email address so we can greet you, and what is in your bag so it is still there when you come back.

To see how the store is doing, we count visits ourselves, without cookies and without any outside analytics service. We count which pages are viewed, the website you came from (for example Instagram or Google, not the page itself), whether you are on a phone, tablet or computer, how quickly pages load, and whether a visit added something to the bag or placed an order. A random number that your browser keeps only until you close the tab joins up the pages of one visit, and we do not keep it once the visit ends. We do not store your IP address, and these counts do not tell us who you are. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or "do not track" signal, we do not count your visit.

We do not use advertising cookies or outside tracking and analytics tools. If that ever changes, we will update this policy first.

News and offers

We send news and offers only to people who have joined our mailing list. You can stop them at any time by replying to one of our emails or writing to us at contact.cuddleleaf@gmail.com. Messages about an order you have placed are not marketing, so we will still send those.

How we keep it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access:

  • Your password is never stored as you typed it. We keep only a scrambled version that cannot be turned back into your password, so not even we can see it.
  • Access to customer and order information is limited to the people who need it to run the store.
  • Backup copies we keep away from our own systems are encrypted and cannot be opened without a separate password.

No website can promise perfect security. Please choose a password you do not use anywhere else, and tell us straight away if you think someone else has used your account.

How long we keep it

  • Your account stays open until you ask us to close it.
  • Order records and invoices are kept for as long as the law requires us to keep business and tax records, even if you close your account.
  • Reviews stay on the website until you ask us to remove them or the product is no longer sold.
  • Mailing list details are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your choices

You can:

  • see your orders and messages, and update your name, email, mobile number and saved addresses, in your account;
  • change your password at any time, or reset it if you forget it;
  • ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct anything that is wrong or out of date;
  • ask us to close your account and delete what we are not required to keep.

To do any of these, write to us at contact.cuddleleaf@gmail.com. We may need to check it is really you first, and we aim to reply within 30 days. There is no charge for asking.

Children

Our products are for babies, but this website is for adults. You need to be 18 or over to create an account or place an order, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Questions and complaints

If you have a question or are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please write to us at contact.cuddleleaf@gmail.com and we will do our best to put it right. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at www.oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the store grows, for example when online payment opens. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last changed. If a change is significant, we will make that clear on this website.
